2. The eSAKSHI portal
• Launched on 1 April 2023 by MoSPI as an end-to-end solution for the MPLAD Scheme.
• Login credentials for all stakeholders: MPs, MoSPI, State Nodal Departments, District Authorities and
Implementing Agencies.
• MPs can recommend works online and through a mobile app, 'on the go'.
• District Authorities examine and sanction works, designate Implementing Agencies and monitor
progress.
• Implementing Agencies upload photographs and documents (sanction orders, images of assets) at
various stages.
• A public dashboard shows works recommended, sanctioned, in progress and completed, with funds
status.
• The portal enforces timelines (e.g. 45 days to accept/reject, 75 days to sanction) through tracking and
alerts.

Less than 3 months Nil
Up to 9 months 50% of the annual allocation
More than 9 months 100% of the annual allocation
On the sudden death or resignation of an MP, works already duly recommended and sanctioned must still be
completed. The incoming MP's entitlement starts afresh. Check the 2023 Guidelines for any change to this table under
the single-instalment system.
4. What happens to unspent balances?
• Funds are non-lapsable: unspent money stays available and is carried forward.
• Lok Sabha: uncommitted balance left by the predecessor passes to the successor MP of the same
constituency.
• Elected Rajya Sabha: uncommitted balance is distributed equally among successor/sitting Rajya
Sabha MPs of that State by the State Government.
• Nominated MPs: balances are distributed among successor nominated MPs with the Government of
India's approval.
• Premature vacancy (death/resignation): the new MP is treated as the successor and the balance goes
to the successor's account.
• Interest earned: under the 2016 guidelines it could be used for permissible works; under the revised
rules reported in 2021-23, MPs can no longer use accumulated interest for works. Verify in the current
Guidelines.
• Savings on completed works are refunded by the Implementing Agency within 30 days.
5. Accounts, utilisation certificates and audit
• MP-wise accounts are maintained for MPLADS funds; deposit in treasury accounts and fixed deposits
are not allowed (older guidelines).
• Utilisation Certificate (UC) - furnished annually by the District Authority to the State and MoSPI
(Annexure-VIII format in the 2016 text).
• Audit Certificate - audited by Chartered Accountants/local fund auditors/statutory auditors engaged by
the State on the Accountant General's recommendation; due by 30 September every year with replies
to audit objections (2016 text).
• Audit reports are MP-wise and check: bank accounts, fixed deposits, interest utilisation, monthly bank
reconciliation, diversion of funds, prohibited works and SC/ST fund utilisation.
• CAG carries out test audits; the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha MPLADS Committees review
implementation.
• Older fund-release criteria: second instalment depended on UCs and audit certificates for the previous
year, an unsanctioned balance below Rs. 1 crore, and unspent balance below Rs. 2.5 crore. The 2023
single-instalment procedure replaces these two-instalment conditions.
6. Monitoring and inspection
Level Monitoring mandate
MoSPI Monitors funds released, works sanctioned and expenditure; tracks completion reports, UCs
and audit certificates; annual report; reviews in States and at the Centre at least yearly;
reviews SC/ST utilisation and calamity rehabilitation works.
State/UT Review committee under the Chief Secretary at least once a year; officers inspect works
during field visits; State nodal officials inspect at least 1% of works in each district yearly;
third-party physical and quality audit (all works costing Rs. 25 lakh and above, 50% of works
costing Rs. 15-25 lakh, plus samples).
District Inspects at least 10% of ongoing works each year; monthly/quarterly review with agencies;
inspection registers for trust/society works.
Implementing Agency Inspects 100% of its works; monthly progress reports.
7. Penal provisions
The 2023 Guidelines introduced, for the first time, a penal clause for material breach of the Guidelines.
Abandoned or suspended works must be completed from State Government funds with responsibility fixed
on erring officials (2016 text). Trusts/societies that misuse assets can be required to return costs with 18%
interest.
